Questions guests ask us

Answers for both homes. Where the Yellow Brick Home and the lake condo differ, we've said so.

Booking

Is booking here different from Airbnb or Vrbo?

It's the same home, the same cleaning and the same hosts. You book straight with us through a secure checkout, and your dates block off on every site at once, so there's no risk of a double booking. The difference is price: our nightly rates here are lower than on Airbnb and Vrbo, with no booking service fee.

Do you have promo codes?

Yes. We share discount codes on our social media (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok) from time to time, so check there for current promos. Enter your code at checkout on the booking page.

What's your cancellation policy?

Each home has its own policy, matching the one it uses on Airbnb.

The Yellow Brick Home (Moderate)

  • Full refund if you cancel at least 5 days before check-in.
  • No refund for cancellations less than 5 days before check-in.

The lake condo (Balanced)

  • Full refund if you cancel at least 30 days before check-in.
  • 50% refund if you cancel 7 to 30 days before check-in.
  • No refund for cancellations less than 7 days before check-in.

At both homes, if you book at least 7 days ahead, you can also cancel for a full refund within 24 hours of booking. Your policy is shown at checkout before you pay.

Do you take longer stays?

Yes, both homes welcome longer stays. The Yellow Brick Home is popular with traveling nurses, legislators and working professionals. Send us a message before booking a long stay and we'll help you plan it.

Is there an age requirement?

Yes. At both homes, the person booking must be 25 or older.

How do I reach you?

Email us at zjpproperties@gmail.com. Once you've booked, you can also message us through your booking confirmation. We live nearby and answer quickly.

Arrival & departure

What are check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is after 3:00 pm and check-out is by 10:00 am at both homes. If you need a little flexibility, ask us. We'll help when the calendar allows.

How do I get in?

Both homes have self check-in with a keypad or smart lock. Your personal door code arrives with your check-in details and works only during your stay. At the Yellow Brick Home, it opens both the front and basement doors.

What do I need to do at check-out?

Just the basics: start the dishwasher, gather used towels, take out the trash, turn things off and lock up. Each home's page has its short checklist.

Parking

Where do I park at the Yellow Brick Home?

There are two off-street spaces behind the house: one covered under the deck, one beside it. The driveway is shared with the neighbors, so please don't block it for long.

Where do I park at the lake condo?

Parking at Lighthouse Landing is free, first come first served. On holiday and special-event weekends (Memorial Day, July 4th, Labor Day), the association allows 2 vehicles per condo, each with a dash pass. We leave the passes in the condo. Please leave them there when you go. Vehicles without passes are towed.

Can I bring a boat or jet ski trailer?

Trailers can't be parked anywhere at Lighthouse Landing. You can launch at a nearby boat ramp and keep your boat in our 12' × 32' slip. Message us for launch and trailer parking suggestions.

The lake condo

Is the boat slip included?

Yes. A 12' × 32' boat slip comes with every stay at no extra cost. No fueling, gas cans or other flammables are allowed on the docks.

Why do I have to sign a form before arriving?

The Lighthouse Landing condo association requires every renter to sign its renters' agreement (name, email, phone, unit number, stay dates and signature) and requires us to register your vehicle. It's used only to reach you in an emergency. We'll send you the link after you book.

When is the pool open?

The heated and chilled pool is open from May through October, weather permitting, 9 am to 11 pm. No glass and no smoking at the pool, and kids under 14 need a parent or guardian with them. There's also a swim dock on Dock 2.

Are there quiet hours?

Yes, 11 pm to 7 am across the complex. Lighthouse Landing is a quiet, family-friendly community, so parties of any kind (including bachelor and bachelorette parties) aren't allowed.

What happens if association rules are broken?

The association fines the unit for renter violations, starting at $250, and can remove renters from the property. Glass at the pool and fueling on the docks bring an immediate fine. These fines are set by the association, not by us.

House rules

Can I bring my dog?

We're sorry, pets aren't allowed at either home.

At the lake, you may see dogs around Lighthouse Landing. They belong to condo owners. The association doesn't allow renters to bring pets, and it removes renters found with one and fines $200 a day.

Is smoking allowed?

No smoking or vaping inside either home. At the Yellow Brick Home, smoking is allowed outside. At the lake condo, the designated smoking area is by the stairs at the parking lot.

Are kids welcome?

Absolutely. The Yellow Brick Home has a Pack 'n Play, high chair, kids' dinnerware, books and toys. The lake condo has a travel crib, stair gates and kids' dinnerware.

How many guests can stay?

Up to 4 overnight guests at the Yellow Brick Home (a Jefferson City ordinance) and up to 6 at the lake condo.
