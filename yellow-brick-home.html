A quiet base for your time in Jefferson City

The Yellow Brick Home is a newly renovated two-bedroom house on a quiet residential street, 1.5 miles from the State Capitol and downtown. Outside of the morning and afternoon commute, the street stays peaceful.

Families like the fenced backyard and the kid-friendly extras. Traveling nurses, legislators and working professionals use the desk and fast WiFi. Katy Trail cyclists come back year after year.

You'll have the whole house to yourself: the main floor, the basement, the fenced yard and the deck. We live in the neighborhood, so we're close if you need anything, but we'll leave you to enjoy your stay.

Arriving & parking

Self check-in. Both the front door and the basement door have keypads. You'll get your personal code with your check-in details, and it works only during your stay.

Parking. There are two off-street spaces behind the house: one covered under the deck, one beside it. The driveway is shared with the house next door, so please don't block it for long.

House rules

  • Check-in after 3:00 pm, check-out by 10:00 am
  • Up to 4 overnight guests (city ordinance)
  • The person booking must be 25 or older
  • Kids and infants welcome
  • No pets
  • No smoking inside; smoking is allowed outside
  • No parties or events

Before you go

  • Load and start the dishwasher (pods are under the sink)
  • Put used towels in the tub
  • Bag your trash and put it in the large can under the deck
  • Turn off lights and appliances, close and lock the windows
  • Close the door and press "Lock" on the keypad. There are no keys to return.
