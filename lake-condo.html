Quiet cove mornings, Bagnell Dam evenings

Our condo sits near the back of a peaceful no-wake cove at Lighthouse Landing. It's the largest two-bedroom, two-bath unit in the complex, with a view of the water from the living room, the primary bedroom and the screened-in balcony.

Have coffee on the balcony, swim off the dock, then head five minutes up the road to the restaurants and nightlife around Bagnell Dam. Bring your boat: a 12' × 32' slip comes with your stay.

We've stocked it so you only need to bring luggage and groceries. All linens and towels are provided, plus starter supplies of dish detergent, toilet paper and paper towels.

Arriving & parking

Self check-in. The front door has a smart lock. You'll get your personal code with your check-in details, and it works only during your stay.

Easy access. Ours is the unit closest to the parking lot, just 6 steps down to the walkway. Coming down the hill, stay right, go past the pool, and look for Building 5 on the left just past the garages.

Parking. Free parking, first come first served, with no assigned spaces. On holiday and special-event weekends (Memorial Day, July 4th, Labor Day), the association allows 2 vehicles per condo, each with a dash pass. We leave both passes in the condo for you.

No trailers at Lighthouse Landing Boat and jet ski trailers can't be parked anywhere on the property. Message us and we'll point you to nearby options.

Lighthouse Landing requirements

Renters' agreement required before arrival The condo association requires every renter to sign its renters' agreement before check-in: name, email, phone, unit number, stay dates and signature. It also requires us to register your vehicle. The association uses this only to reach you in an emergency. We'll send you the form after you book.
  • Check-in after 3:00 pm, check-out by 10:00 am
  • Up to 6 guests; the person booking must be 25 or older
  • Quiet hours on the complex: 11 pm to 7 am
  • No parties, including bachelor and bachelorette parties
  • No pets. Dogs you see around the complex belong to condo owners; the association doesn't allow renters to bring pets.
  • No smoking or vaping inside. The smoking area is by the stairs at the parking lot.
  • Pool: 9 am to 11 pm, no glass, no smoking; kids under 14 with an adult
  • Docks: no fueling, gas cans or flammables; kids 10 and under need an adult and a life jacket
  • Exterior security cameras on the complex grounds

The association fines the unit when renters break its rules, starting at $250, and can remove renters from the property. Those fines are set by the association, not by us.

Before you go

  • Load and start the dishwasher, and turn off the ice maker
  • Leave used towels and washcloths in the shower
  • Take trash to the Lighthouse Landing dumpster on the right as you leave the complex
  • Leave the parking passes in the condo
  • Turn everything off, close the windows and lock the door
