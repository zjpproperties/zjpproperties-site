Things to Do in Jefferson City & Lake of the Ozarks

We're Jessica and Zach, and we host guests in both places: The Yellow Brick Home in Jefferson City and our lakeside condo near Bagnell Dam. They're about 40 minutes apart, so plenty of our guests do a little of both. Here's what we tell friends who visit.

Jefferson City: Missouri's capital

Jefferson City sits on the bluffs of the Missouri River in the middle of the state. It's an easy base for state business, a weekend on the Katy Trail, or a game day in Columbia.

Missouri State Capitol

The Capitol dome is the centerpiece of downtown, and the building houses the Missouri State Museum. It's about 1.5 miles from the Yellow Brick Home. During the legislative session, roughly January through mid-May, downtown is busy with legislators, staff and visitors, so book early if your trip lines up with it.

Katy Trail State Park

The Katy Trail is a flat crushed-stone rail-trail that runs across much of Missouri along the Missouri River. Trailheads near Jefferson City are a short drive from the house, and cyclists like having a quiet, comfortable place to rest between rides. Guests ride out and back in a day or stop here on longer trips across the state.

Missouri State Penitentiary tours

The decommissioned Missouri State Penitentiary, once one of the oldest prisons operating west of the Mississippi, offers guided history and ghost tours. It's one of the most popular things to do in town, and tours can sell out on weekends.

JC River Market

Jefferson City's newest riverfront park opened in September 2026 next to the old penitentiary, at 160 N. Chestnut St. Its glass-and-brick pavilion sits near the Missouri River, with food trucks, a farmers market and community events, plus a lighted "JCMO" sign that makes a great photo stop. It pairs well with a penitentiary tour, and it's a short drive from the Yellow Brick Home.

Downtown, parks and food

High Street downtown has local restaurants, coffee and shops within walking distance of the Capitol. Closer to the Yellow Brick Home, Memorial Park has a walking trail, playgrounds and a family aquatic center in summer, and West Main Pizza is a short walk away.

Columbia and Mizzou

Columbia and the University of Missouri are about 35 minutes north. Jefferson City is a quieter option than staying in Columbia on football and graduation weekends.

Lake of the Ozarks

Lake of the Ozarks is one of the Midwest's favorite lake getaways, with miles of winding shoreline, coves and lake-front restaurants. Our condo is at Lighthouse Landing in Lake Ozark, five minutes from Bagnell Dam.

Bagnell Dam and the Bagnell Dam Strip

Bagnell Dam formed the lake, and the Strip next to it is the lake's classic stretch of restaurants, bars, arcades and shops. It's about five minutes from the condo, which makes it easy to get dinner and be back on the balcony for the evening.

Boating, swimming and fishing

The lake is made for days on the water. The condo comes with a 12' × 32' boat slip, a swim dock and a quiet no-wake cove for swimming and fishing. If you don't bring a boat, you can rent one from marinas around the lake.

Lake-front dining by boat or car

Many lake restaurants have docks, so you can arrive by car or by boat. Favorites near the condo include Neon Taco and the Boathouse Lakeside Bar & Grill (5 to 7 minutes by car), and Jolly Rogers Grub & Grogg is worth the trip (about 20 minutes by car). All three have great views of the lake. For a nicer dinner out, JB Hooks (about 11 minutes by car) also has great lake views. For pizza, Alley Cats Pizzeria is about 5 minutes away by car.

State parks and caves

Lake of the Ozarks State Park is Missouri's largest state park, with hiking, beaches and caves. Ha Ha Tonka State Park, with its castle ruins on a bluff over the lake, is a scenic drive away and one of the most photographed spots in the area. The region also has show caves you can tour year-round.

Golf and the off-season

The lake has a long list of golf courses, and spring and fall are quieter, cheaper times to visit. The condo's pool runs May through October, and the balcony view is good in any season.
