Privacy policy

Effective November 2026

ZJP Properties is operated by J&Z Rentals LLC in Jefferson City, Missouri. We rent The Yellow Brick Home in Jefferson City and our condo at Lighthouse Landing in Lake Ozark. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit this site or book a stay with us, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Booking details: your name, email, phone number, stay dates, number of guests and anything you tell us in messages.
  • Payment details: payments are handled by our booking provider, Hospitable, and its payment partners. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Lake condo only: the Lighthouse Landing condo association requires a signed renters' agreement (name, email, phone, unit number, stay dates and signature) and your vehicle information.
  • Emails you send us at zjpproperties@gmail.com.

How we use it

  • To confirm and manage your reservation, send check-in details and your door code, and answer your questions.
  • To process payments, refunds and required lodging taxes.
  • To keep our homes and guests safe and to meet local and association rules.
  • Occasionally, to let past guests know about availability or offers. You can ask us to stop at any time.

Who we share it with

  • Hospitable, the platform that runs our booking calendar, checkout and guest messaging, and its payment partners.
  • The Lighthouse Landing condo association, for lake condo stays only. The association uses your information to reach you in an emergency and to manage parking.
  • Cleaning and maintenance help, only the dates and details they need to prepare the home.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it.

We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with advertisers.

Cookies and outside services

This site doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. Our fonts load from Google Fonts, and the booking calendar is provided by Hospitable, which may use cookies to run checkout. Their privacy policies cover the information they handle.

How long we keep it

We keep reservation records for as long as we need them for tax, accounting and legal purposes, then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where we're not required to keep it. Email zjpproperties@gmail.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

Children

Bookings must be made by an adult 25 or older. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we update this policy, we'll post the new version here with a new effective date.
